FHMIS Secure Backup

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

FHMIS Secure Backup is a backup solution being configured for the Forest Harvest Management Information System (FHMIS).

These terms describe the intended use of the backup integration and its associated information website.

2. Authorized Use

The backup integration is intended for use by authorized FHMIS administrators only.

Unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or distribution of protected FHMIS information is not permitted.

3. Backup and Recovery

The planned backup workflow is designed to preserve authorized FHMIS development files, configuration records, and recovery information.

Backup integrity and restoration procedures must be tested before operational use.

4. Google Drive Integration

The planned integration will use Google Drive access authorized by the account owner for backup storage and recovery.

Access may be revoked through the connected Google Account.

5. Data Protection

Backup archives are intended to be encrypted before uploading to Google Drive. Encryption credentials must be stored separately and securely.

6. Service Availability

The backup integration is currently being configured and is not yet an operational backup service.

Availability may depend on internet connectivity, Google Drive service availability, and the local backup environment.

7. Responsibilities

Authorized administrators are responsible for protecting credentials, monitoring backup results, and testing restoration procedures.

8. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated to reflect changes in the backup workflow, security requirements, or applicable policies.

9. Contact

For questions about FHMIS Secure Backup, contact the administrator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
